It's all just caffeine delivery.

Strip the branding off an energy drink and what's left is caffeine, sugar water, and a marketing budget. The caffeine itself is one of the cheapest substances in the store — how you choose to take it in changes the price by more than 20× for a NoDoz tablet specifically, and the cheapest bulk pill is ~118× cheaper than Monster per 100mg.     So what does the extra money buy?

    A Monster costs about $2.24 per 100mg of caffeine; a NoDoz tablet costs about $0.11 per 100mg (bulk generic pills are ~$0.02 per 100mg). That difference isn't buying more energy — it's buying the stuff around the caffeine. Here's what the science says about the supporting cast, following the Scientific American rundown:

    The one that works

    Caffeine

    The only ingredient with solid evidence behind it. It blocks adenosine, you feel alert. Everything on this page delivers the same molecule.

    Mostly decoration

    Taurine

    An amino acid your body already makes and gets from food. Reviews have repeatedly failed to find an energy effect at the doses in a can.

    Mostly decoration

    B vitamins

    B12 and friends help turn food into energy if you're deficient. If you're not, the excess is simply excreted — expensive urine, as the saying goes.

    Mostly decoration

    Ginseng & guarana

    Ginseng's evidence for alertness is weak at can-sized doses. Guarana is real, but only because it's just more caffeine — from a plant instead of a lab.

    Real, but a rollercoaster

    Sugar

    54g in a regular 16oz Monster. It does give quick energy — and a quick crash. Sugar-free versions skip it, but then the can is even more plainly just caffeine.

    The honest extra

    The ritual

    A cold can, a flavor you like, a break in your day. That's genuinely worth something to a lot of people — just know it's the part you're paying 20× for.

    Keep the total in view: the FDA cites 400mg of caffeine a day as the amount not generally associated with negative effects for healthy adults. That's 2½ Monsters — or, at NoDoz tablet prices, about 44 cents' worth. However you take it in, the ceiling is the same.
