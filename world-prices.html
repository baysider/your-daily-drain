Three things that jump out

Europe's secret is the big can

Monster is a 16.9oz (500ml) can almost everywhere in Europe vs 16oz (473ml) in the US — and it still costs less. Per ounce, a German Monster works out at roughly half the US price.

China's Red Bull isn't the same drink

The gold can sold in China is a licensed, non-carbonated version with a different formula. It's also why it's so cheap — about ¥6. Don't compare it like-for-like without that note.

Every market has a local spoiler

Burn in Italy, ZONe in Japan, HOT6 in Korea, Eastroc in China. The local brand is often the cheapest can on the shelf, but by how much varies a lot — about 17% under Red Bull for Eastroc in China, about 13% under for Burn in Italy. ZONe is the exception: in Japan it actually costs slightly more than Red Bull.

How to read this page: Prices from the November 2026 store-by-store research (Tesco in the UK, Knuspr in Germany, Carrefour in France and Spain, HKTVmall in Hong Kong, FairPrice in Singapore, and equivalent supermarket listings elsewhere). Single-can prices, not multipacks — multipacks are cheaper everywhere. USD figures are converted from the local price at the ECB reference rates for 6 Nov 2026 and kept at full precision — only the number on display is rounded, and yearly costs are worked out from the unrounded price. The rates: €1 = $1.1165, £1 = $1.3386; $1 = ¥158.48 (Japan), ₩1,363.80, ¥6.675 (China), HK$7.84, S$1.2826. Deposits (Pfand in Germany and similar schemes) not included. Can sizes differ by market, so prices are shown per can with the size in ounces, plus cents per ounce for the fairest comparison.

Key spot-checks you can verify: UK Red Bull 250ml £2.10, Monster 500ml £2.10 and Rockstar 500ml £1.30 at Tesco; France Red Bull 250ml €1.19 and Monster 500ml €1.35 at Carrefour (fresh check, Nov 8 — the €2.07 Red Bull figure seen earlier was a Carrefour Express delivery markup, not the shelf price); Japan Red Bull 250ml ¥213 before tax (¥230 with tax) and Monster 355ml ¥230 with tax; Germany Red Bull, Monster and Rockstar all €1.59 at Knuspr. Cells marked “price to verify” — France Rockstar, China Monster, Korea HOT6, Singapore Red Bull — have no verified shelf price and are deliberately shown without one. Cells flagged research-weak (Italy Monster, Singapore's Krating Daeng, Korea's two prices) rest on a single weaker source and should be re-checked before this page goes live.
